Privacy Policy

Effective 6 November 2026 · Last updated 6 November 2026

In short:

  • This site collects nothing about you. It has no forms, no cookies, no analytics and no trackers, and it loads nothing from other servers.
  • GitHub hosts the site. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security, under its own privacy statement.
  • The page tells a family history. Living relatives are named only with their consent.
  • To ask for a correction or a removal, email ismail@kattakath.com.

Who runs this site

kattakath.com is the Kattakath family page: a history of a Mappila tharavad (ancestral house) name from Kerala. Ismail Kattakath, in Toronto, Canada, keeps it. In this policy, "I" and "me" mean him.

This policy covers the page at kattakath.com and its policy pages. It does not cover the separate apps on kattakath.com subdomains (see below).

What this site collects

Nothing. The page is a single static file. It has no sign-up, no forms, no comments and no scripts. It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. Its fonts and images are built into the file, so your browser makes no requests to any other company when you read it.

I do not run analytics, so I do not know who visits or how often.

Hosting by GitHub

The site is published with GitHub Pages, from the public repository kattakath/kattakath.github.io. Like any web host, GitHub's servers see your request when you load the page.

GitHub states that when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes, whether or not the visitor is signed in to GitHub. GitHub handles that data, not me, and I have no access to it. See GitHub's note on GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Links to other sites

The page links to a few outside sites, such as hopechildcancercare.org, ktcbank.com and github.com/ismailkattakath. When you follow a link, you leave this site, and that site's own privacy policy applies.

People named on this page

A family history is about people. The page describes where the family lived, where its branches moved, and what family members built, such as businesses and charities.

  • Living relatives are named only with their consent. Until someone agrees, the page describes what they built instead of naming them (for example, "Founded by a Kattakath."), and links to their own site where there is one.
  • The keeper, Ismail Kattakath, is the only person the page names today.

If you are named or described on the page, or you speak for a family member who is, you can ask me to correct it, add to it, or take it down.

Corrections and removal

Email ismail@kattakath.com. Say what you want changed and where it appears on the page. I may ask a question to make sure I change the right thing.

Please know one limit. The site is built from a public git repository, so earlier versions of the page stay in its history, and other people or web archives may already have copies. I can change or remove the content on the live page, but I cannot recall copies that others hold.

If you email me

If you write to me, I receive your email address and whatever you choose to send. The kattakath.com mailbox runs on Google's email service. I use your message only to reply and to act on your request. I do not sell it or share it for marketing.

Your rights

You can ask me what personal information about you is on this site or in my email from you, and ask me to correct or delete it. Email ismail@kattakath.com. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to complain to your local privacy authority.

Other apps on kattakath.com

Some apps run on kattakath.com subdomains. Each has its own privacy policy, and that policy governs it, not this one. For example, JSON Resume Connect has its own privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I will update this page and the "Last updated" date at the top. The repository's history shows every earlier version.
