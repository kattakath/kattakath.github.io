Privacy Policy

Effective 6 November 2026 · Last updated 6 November 2026

In short:

  • This policy covers Kattakath Family, the app that lets you sign in with Google to family services and connect Gmail to your AI assistant. It also covers the website kattakath.com.
  • Sign-in uses only your name, email address and Google account ID.
  • The Gmail tool runs on your own computer. It reads, organises, drafts and sends email, and manages Gmail filters and settings, only when you ask your AI assistant to do so. No server I operate receives, relays or stores your email.
  • I do not sell your data. I do not use it for ads. I do not use it to train AI models.
  • You can remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections, and ask me to delete your data by email.
  • Kattakath Family's use of Google user data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

1. Who I am

Kattakath Family is built and run by Ismail Kattakath, an individual developer in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. In this policy, "I", "me" and "my" mean him. "You" means anyone who uses the app or visits the website.

Contact: ismail@kattakath.com.

2. What this policy covers

This policy covers:

  • Kattakath Family sign-in: "Sign in with Google" for family services on kattakath.com that are protected by Cloudflare Access.
  • Kattakath Family AI gateway sign-in: an AI model gateway that you sign in to with Google.
  • Kattakath Family Gmail tool: a desktop tool, running on your own computer, that lets the AI assistant you choose work with your Gmail on your instruction.
  • The website kattakath.com and its policy pages.

Some apps on kattakath.com publish their own privacy policy, and that policy governs them instead of this one. For example, JSON Resume Connect has its own privacy policy.

3. Google user data the app accesses

When you connect a Google account, Google shows you the permissions requested. Each part of the app asks only for what it needs.

Sign-in (Cloudflare Access and the AI gateway) asks only for:

  • openid: your Google account ID, so the service can recognise you.
  • userinfo.email: your email address, and whether Google has verified it.
  • userinfo.profile: your name and profile picture, as you set them in Google.

The Gmail tool may also use the sign-in data above, and asks for:

  • gmail.modify (Google's description: "Read, compose, and send emails from your Gmail account"): your messages, threads, attachments and labels; the ability to search and read them, apply or remove labels, mark them read or unread, move them to or from the trash, create and edit drafts, and send email. This permission cannot permanently delete email in a way that bypasses the trash.
  • gmail.settings.basic (Google's description: "See, edit, create, or change your email settings and filters in Gmail"): your Gmail filters and basic settings, such as the vacation responder, language, and IMAP and POP settings; the ability to view, create, change and delete them.

The Gmail permissions are requested only by the Gmail tool. Signing in to a family service or the AI gateway never gives access to your Gmail.

4. How I use that data

  • Sign-in data is used to confirm who you are, decide whether your account may use the service you are signing in to, and keep you signed in.
  • Gmail data is used only to carry out what you ask your AI assistant to do with your email. For example: "find the invoice from last week", "label these newsletters", "draft a reply" or "create a filter for these senders". The tool acts only on your instruction.
  • I do not use Google user data for any other purpose. I do not use it for advertising, profiling, market research, credit decisions or lending.

5. Your AI assistant and your Gmail

The Gmail tool runs on your own computer. It connects your Gmail to an AI assistant that you choose and connect yourself. Your email goes directly between Google, the tool on your computer and that assistant. It never passes through a server I operate.

  • When you give the assistant a request about your email, the tool passes the email content needed for that request to the assistant's AI model. This is a transfer that you direct, made only to provide the feature you asked for.
  • The assistant uses that content only to carry out your request, such as summarising, sorting, drafting or replying.
  • The tool does not send your email anywhere you have not asked it to.
  • Your AI assistant's provider handles the content it receives under its own terms and privacy policy. Please choose a provider whose terms you accept.
  • I do not use your Gmail data to develop, improve or train any AI or machine-learning model.

6. How data is stored, protected and kept

  • The Gmail tool is on-device only. It runs on your own computer. No server operated by me receives, relays or stores your email content, and I have no copy of it. Your email stays in your Gmail account.
  • Email content is fetched from Gmail on your computer only to carry out your request, and is passed to the AI model you choose only for that request. The tool does not keep its own copy of your email.
  • Gmail access tokens are the only thing the Gmail tool keeps. They stay on your computer, in your operating system's secure credential store. They never reach me.
  • Protection. Google user data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS) to and from Google's APIs. Each part of the app gets only the access its feature needs.
  • Sign-in records. The services you sign in to keep your name, email address and account ID while you have access, so they can recognise you. They may keep a record of sign-ins for security.
  • Gmail access tokens are kept on your computer until you revoke access or remove the tool. Revoking access makes them useless at once.
  • When data is no longer needed for the purpose it was collected for, I delete it.

7. Sharing and transfer

I do not sell Google user data. I do not share it with advertisers, data brokers or information resellers. I share or transfer it only in these cases:

  • To provide the feature you use. Sign-in data goes to Cloudflare (Cloudflare Access), which checks your identity for family services, or to the AI gateway you sign in to. Gmail content goes to the AI assistant you choose, as explained in section 5.
  • For security, for example to investigate abuse or a security problem.
  • To comply with the law, such as a valid court order.
  • In a transfer of the app to a new owner, and only after asking for your explicit consent first.

No one reads your email. I do not let any person, including me, read your Google user data unless: you first ask me to and agree to it for specific messages (for example, to help with a support request); it is necessary for security, such as investigating a bug or abuse; it is required by law; or the data is aggregated and used for internal operations as the law allows.

8. Limited Use disclosure

Kattakath Family's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Google Workspace API data, including Gmail data, is not used to develop, improve or train generalized (non-personalized) AI and/or ML models.

9. Removing access and deleting your data

  • Remove access. Go to your Google account's third-party connections page, choose Kattakath Family, and remove its access. The app can then no longer reach your Google data.
  • Ask for deletion. Email ismail@kattakath.com from the account concerned, and say what you want deleted. I will delete the Google user data I hold about you, such as sign-in records, unless the law requires me to keep it. I hold no email content and no Gmail tokens: those stay on your computer. Remove them by revoking access and uninstalling the tool. I will confirm by email when it is done.
  • Removing access or deleting data does not delete anything in your Google account itself. Email you sent or labels you applied stay as they are in Gmail.

10. The website kattakath.com

kattakath.com is a static family-heritage page. It has no forms, cookies, analytics or trackers, and it collects nothing about you.

GitHub Pages hosts it. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security, under its own policy. See GitHub's note on GitHub Pages data collection. I have no access to those logs.

The page tells a family history. Living relatives are named only with their consent. To ask for a correction or a removal, email ismail@kattakath.com. I can change the live page. Earlier versions stay in its public git history, and web archives may hold copies I cannot recall.

The page links to other websites. Their own privacy policies apply when you visit them.

11. Children

The services are not directed to children, and I do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has given me data, email ismail@kattakath.com and I will delete it.

12. Your rights

You can ask me what personal information I hold about you, and ask me to correct or delete it. Email ismail@kattakath.com. Depending on where you live, you may also complain to your privacy authority. In Canada, that is the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

13. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I will update this page and the "Last updated" date. If I want to use Google user data in a new way, I will tell you and ask for your consent before I do.
